Who we are

Skylark Star Logistics, LLC was established in 2022 and is based in La Porte, Texas. We provide transportation, logistics, freight handling, and delivery services to businesses that need dependable partners.

The company was formed as a Colorado LLC and is currently registering to operate in Texas.
